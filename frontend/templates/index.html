Research & Innovation

At USETAI, research is central to everything we build. Our interdisciplinary teams explore the frontier of applied AI to deliver trustworthy, high-impact solutions.

Explainable & Responsible AI

Developing interpretable models and governance frameworks so organizations can trust, audit, and explain every AI decision.

Autonomous Agents

Building goal-directed multi-agent systems that reason, plan, and act autonomously in complex enterprise environments.

Scientific Machine Learning

Combining physics-informed neural networks with classical simulation to accelerate scientific discovery and engineering design.

Federated Learning

Enabling privacy-preserving collaborative training across distributed data sources without centralising sensitive information.

AI Safety

Investigating alignment, robustness, and red-teaming techniques to ensure large-scale AI systems remain safe and controllable.

Generative AI Research

Advancing retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning, and evaluation benchmarks for domain-specific language models.
